# Rehearsal Monitor

Welcome to Rehearsal Monitor! Easily see how rehearsal time is spent!

Rehearsal Monitor is a tool that classifies audio segments into one of the following categories:
- Teacher Instruction
- Music Performance
- Student Talking
- Silence

No chess clock. No manual timing. All automated.

Rehearsal Monitor is perfect for student teachers, young music-educators, and even musicians themselves needing to see how much time is spent talking vs. making music.

## Developer notes: 

As of the initial release, Rehearsal Monitor is only available for iOS.

Rehearsal Monitor works best when the phone is placed in front of an ensemble on a conductor stand.

The app has been tested in multiple rehearsal settings. If you see an audio sample being misclassified, please send an email to [support@cuetechniques.com](mailto:support@cuetechniques.com?subject=Rehearsal Monitor - Misclassification") and I'll make an update.
